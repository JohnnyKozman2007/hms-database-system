# Presentation & Live Database Demonstration

This folder holds presentation slides and demo notes for the final team presentation.

## Presentation Requirements (Section 20 of Brief)
- Time allocated: typically 10–15 minutes + Q&A.
- Every student must speak and be ready to answer questions about the entire integrated system, not just their individual 3 tables.

---

## 📑 Slide Deck Structure (10–12 Slides)
1. **Slide 1: Title & Team Members** (Names, IDs, Assigned Roles).
2. **Slide 2: Problem Statement & System Overview** (Hospital context, 16 entities).
3. **Slide 3: Integrated ERD** (Full diagram showing all connections and cardinalities).
4. **Slide 4: Relational Schema & 3NF Normalization** (Atomic values, partial dependency removal, transitive dependency removal).
5. **Slide 5: Staff & Department Management (Student 1)** (Key queries and insights).
6. **Slide 6: Patient & Admission Management (Student 2)** (Room occupancy and patient admissions).
7. **Slide 7: Appointments & Clinical Procedures (Student 3)** (Doctor scheduling and medical records).
8. **Slide 8: Pharmacy & Lab Management (Student 4)** (Medication tracking and prescriptions).
9. **Slide 9: Financial & Insurance Management (Student 5)** (Invoicing, payments, and supplier links).
10. **Slide 10: Advanced Features & Database Automation** (Showcase of Triggers, Stored Procedures, Functions).
11. **Slide 11: Testing & Data Integrity** (Positive and negative test results, constraint verification).
12. **Slide 12: Conclusion & Q&A**.

---

## 💻 Live Database Demonstration Checklist
- Have Oracle APEX open to SQL Commands / Object Browser.
- Run 2–3 selected queries live to show fast response and clean formatted output.
- Fire an advanced trigger or stored procedure live (e.g. updating a status or calculating a balance) to impress the assessors.
